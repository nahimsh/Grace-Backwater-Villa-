Welcome to Grace Backwater Villa

A Private Sanctuary
Between Hills & Water

Set against the misty hills of Coorg, Grace Backwater Villa is a fully private retreat where the everyday noise fades and nature takes over. Wake up to birdsong, sip filter coffee on your balcony overlooking still backwaters, and spend evenings around a crackling campfire with the people who matter most.

Whether you're planning a family holiday, a reunion with old friends, or a quiet escape as a couple, the villa is designed to feel entirely your own — spacious, comfortable, and thoughtfully cared for by our on-ground team.

  • Surrounded by nature & total privacy
  • Family-friendly & group-friendly spaces
  • Scenic sunrises over the backwaters
  • Luxury comforts, warm home-style hospitality
